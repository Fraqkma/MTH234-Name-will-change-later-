## Candidate-specific leakage, schema, and splits

### 1. Airfoil — recommended

Raw `airfoil_self_noise.dat` is tab-delimited without a header. The exact ordered columns are `frequency` (Hz), `attack-angle` (degrees), `chord-length` (m), `free-stream-velocity` (m/s), `suction-side-displacement-thickness` (m), and **`scaled-sound-pressure` (dB)**. The first five are numeric; the target is continuous. The raw file contains 21 distinct frequencies, 27 angles, 6 chord lengths, 4 velocities, and 105 thickness values. Thickness is constant within each of the 106 angle/chord/velocity groups.

**Prediction-time boundary:** retain `frequency`, `attack-angle`, `chord-length`, and `free-stream-velocity`. Exclude `suction-side-displacement-thickness` from predictors because the publisher does not establish that this measured aerodynamic quantity is known *before* the acoustic prediction. Keep it only for audit/provenance. No target-derived predictor or ID column exists. There are no duplicate predictor rows across all five published inputs; the related frequency sweeps are the important near-duplicates. The experiment/session identity, acquisition time, and whether similar settings share a physical blade are **unverified**. Grouping by the three setting columns is therefore a defensible available proxy, not proof of independent experiments.

**Proposed split:** form stable group keys from the raw text fields (`attack-angle`, `chord-length`, `free-stream-velocity`), sort the distinct key tuples, and use Python `random.Random(42).shuffle` to seal the first **21 groups** (about 20%) as one holdout. This preliminary key-level allocation has **290 rows** in the holdout and 1,213 development rows. The count is structural only; no target-based score or distribution check was used. Five group-disjoint CV folds on the other **85 groups** have about 17 groups each. Use ten predetermined CV seeds **42–51**, sharing the same fixed holdout across every method; the seeds reshuffle development groups among folds. Report the ten repeated-CV estimates as correlated sensitivity checks, not ten independent datasets or ten holdout scores. Fit encoding/scaling and all model coefficients inside each training fold; average validation error by observation, retain per-fold/per-seed values, and choose one frozen model/penalty under a stated tie rule before final holdout evaluation.

Four retained numeric inputs give **15**, **35**, and **70** columns including intercept for full polynomial degrees 2, 3, and 4 respectively (`C(p+d,d)` for `p=4`). Degree 2–3 is a manageable shared search range for OLS, Ridge, and Lasso on ~1,213 development rows; degree 4 may be diagnostic only because the effective independent units are 85 settings and OLS conditioning may suffer. Standardize polynomial non-intercept columns on each training fold only. Do not carry the synthetic demo's degree 1–15 range into this multivariate comparison. Lasso needs a documented penalty normalization and a solver dependency/implementation decision before the protocol freeze.

**Paired method contract:** use identical rows, grouped fold IDs, polynomial basis, training-only scaling, and validation metric (MSE in dB²; report RMSE in dB) for all three methods. OLS has no penalty; Ridge and Lasso tune penalties on the same development folds. State how each penalty relates to the mean or summed residual loss so their numeric labels are not mistaken as directly comparable. Freeze a degree/penalty-selection rule and final refit rule before any holdout scoring. These are proposed evaluation rules, not completed model results.

### 2. Capital Bikeshare — fallback

Use only `hour.csv` (not the aggregate `day.csv`). Exact raw columns are `instant`, `dteday`, `season`, `yr`, `mnth`, `hr`, `holiday`, `weekday`, `workingday`, `weathersit`, `temp`, `atemp`, `hum`, `windspeed`, `casual`, `registered`, `cnt`. The numeric target **`cnt`** counts rentals in that hour. **Exclude `casual` and `registered`** because they sum exactly to the target, and exclude `instant` (row index). Keep `dteday` solely as split/time metadata; derived calendar indicators can be predictors if their definitions are frozen. `season`, `yr`, `mnth`, `hr`, `weekday`, `holiday`, `workingday`, and `weathersit` are categorical/binary; `temp`, `atemp`, `hum`, `windspeed` are numeric normalized weather fields as defined by the publisher. No literal missing values or duplicate rows were found. Neighboring hours and same-day records are correlated even though date-hour keys are unique.

For a genuine **future-hour** test, reserve the last ~20% of *complete dates* as one sealed holdout, with all earlier dates as development. Use five chronological expanding-window folds on the development period, each validation block later than its training block; put all hours of a date on one side of each boundary. Weather variables are only valid predictors if a forecast available before the target hour is supplied; the file has observed weather, not forecast issue times. A strict advance-forecast protocol should **exclude `weathersit`, `temp`, `atemp`, `hum`, and `windspeed`** unless the prediction question is changed to a nowcast after weather observation. This availability claim cannot be settled from the file. Ten differently seeded *random* repeats would be invalid; ten prespecified rolling origins or training-window perturbations could be meaningful but require explicit approval and would have unequal calendar/season coverage. Five sequential folds are feasible in row count but not exchangeable or independent.

One-hot encoding of calendar categories can easily yield roughly 50–60 columns before interactions (24 hour, 12 month, 7 weekday, 4 season, 4 weather, plus binary/year fields). A full degree-2 polynomial over 50 columns has **1,326** terms including intercept and degree 3 has **23,426**, which is too large for a simple local three-model teaching app. Limit interactions to a declared small numeric/calendar subset or additive terms; fit any encoding only on training windows. The 50-column count is a design estimate, not a measured encoded matrix.

### 3. Seoul Bike Sharing Demand

The raw CSV has `Date`, **`Rented Bike Count`**, `Hour`, `Temperature(°C)`, `Humidity(%)`, `Wind speed (m/s)`, `Visibility (10m)`, `Dew point temperature(°C)`, `Solar Radiation (MJ/m2)`, `Rainfall(mm)`, `Snowfall (cm)`, `Seasons`, `Holiday`, and `Functioning Day` (the CSV is Latin-1 encoded). The target is a numeric hourly bike count. Exclude `Date` from the design matrix except for calendar features known before prediction; exclude `Functioning Day` unless a documented advance service schedule proves availability. Its perfect zero-count association on nonfunctioning hours makes it a dangerous proxy. Same-day rows are repeated temporal observations. The raw file has no station/entity ID, no missing cells, and no exact duplicate date-hour.

Reserve the last ~20% of complete dates as a sealed future holdout and use five earlier expanding-window validation folds with whole-day boundaries. Five folds are possible, but the first training window sees only a fraction of the single annual cycle. Ten random seeds cannot create ten independent valid future tests; rolling origins or training-window sensitivity checks would need a protocol change. Same-hour meteorological measurements may be unavailable at forecast time, so either exclude them for an advance forecast or explicitly change the question to a nowcast. The exact weather measurement/issue timing is **unverified**.

After encoding 24 hour categories, 4 seasons, 2 holiday states, and numeric weather variables, a 35–45 column matrix is plausible; full degree 2 creates **666–1,081** columns including intercept, and degree 3 **8,436–17,296**. These are illustrative combinatorial bounds based on the raw category counts, not a fitted encoder. Restrict interactions to a small predeclared subset if selected.

## Recommendation and decision needed

**Recommend Airfoil Self-Noise** with the four preprediction setting/frequency inputs and the fixed grouped split above. The decisive trade-off is **split quality versus claim scope**: grouping prevents frequencies from the same recorded test setting crossing development and holdout, while missing session/batch IDs prevent a stronger claim about new physical experiments. It gives a small, directly comparable polynomial OLS/Ridge/Lasso problem with five grouped folds and ten meaningful seeded development-fold assignments. The final test means *unseen recorded angle/chord/velocity combinations from this dataset*.

**Fallback: Capital Bikeshare hourly demand** if Nipitpon prefers a civic prediction story and accepts a chronological evaluation with a revised ten-run interpretation plus a firm weather-availability choice. Seoul is less suitable because its single year weakens seasonal validation and `Functioning Day` is a strong proxy for zero rentals.

**Open eligibility points:** Airfoil batch/session metadata and suction-thickness availability are not supplied; excluding thickness avoids the latter. Bike weather forecast availability is not established. The raw-file audit cannot verify publisher collection claims or hidden missing-value codes beyond empty/nonfinite checks. None of these points warrants opening a holdout score or choosing a model during dataset selection.

**Requested approval:** Nipitpon should confirm Airfoil, the four retained predictors, and the fixed 21-group holdout with five grouped CV folds repeated under seeds 42–51. This treats ten runs as development-fold sensitivity repeats while keeping one official holdout sealed. If the requirement means ten independent future/holdout tests instead, the evaluation protocol must be resolved before implementation; do not register a dataset or build its loader until that decision.

## Decision record after audit

On 2026-11-18, the project owner chose **UCI Capital Bike Sharing**, specifically `hour.csv`, over the audit's Airfoil recommendation. The deciding benefit is a concrete, understandable presentation question about rentals in a given hour. The team accepts a chronological future-data test and a deliberately small hour × working-day design, which avoids weather-availability assumptions and wide polynomial expansion. This is a project-scope choice, not a conclusion from any candidate's model score. The Airfoil recommendation remains above as the original audit finding.

The approved target is `cnt` (rentals/hour). Predictors are only categorical `hr` and binary `workingday`, with both main effects and their interaction. `dteday` is split metadata. Weather, `casual`, `registered`, `instant`, and every other raw column are excluded from the design. The last 146 distinct dates form the one sealed holdout; five expanding chronological validation windows use earlier dates. The ten-seed and degree-selection studies remain on the synthetic experiment. Exact boundaries, row counts, source fingerprint, and feature contract are in [the bike split contract](bike_split_contract.md). No official holdout score has been calculated.
